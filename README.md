Hi, I'm Daniel 👋 I'm a product engineer and engineering leader in Portland, Oregon. Right now I'm a Staff AI Product Engineer at Blackboard, building a new AI product with Claude Code and Codex in my daily loop.

Most of my career has been software for hotel revenue managers. In 2012 I built a rate-shopping prototype in Excel and VBA. It became [Revcaster](https://hospitalitytech.com/rainmaker-group-acquires-revcaster-llc), which grew into the #1 customer-rated tool on Hotel Tech Report, crawled close to 30 million hotel rates a night, and was acquired by The Rainmaker Group. Since then I've led engineering at Best Western and Trimble, and I still write code every day. I like to say I'm lazy, so I use programming to make my life easier.

## What I've Built

- **Best Western Hotels & Resorts** (2018 to 2025). Led a team of five building for 4,500+ hotels. We shipped Best Western's first production generative AI product: a RAG support assistant on Claude through AWS Bedrock that cut inbound support requests by more than 90%. Before that, I was the lead developer on Best Western's first production app on AWS, which replaced an Excel workflow for 75+ revenue managers across 800 hotels.
- **Trimble** (2026). AI Engineering Manager on enterprise ERP accounting and payroll: ML-powered anomaly detection APIs and getting AI-assisted development into teams' everyday work.
- **Revcaster** (2012 to 2018). Founder and CEO. Excel prototype, then a Rails SaaS platform, then the #1 rated tool in its category, then an acquisition. Remote-first team across North America, Ukraine, and Africa.
- **Wise Revenue Management** (2013 to 2022). Consulting on the side: custom SaaS and serverless tools for 50+ hotel clients.
